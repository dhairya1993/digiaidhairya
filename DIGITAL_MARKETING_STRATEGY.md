## 📌 1. Website SEO Architecture (Done & Implemented)

### ✅ A. On-Page Technical SEO
- **Title Tag**: Optimized with brand and primary search intent (`DigiAi Dhairya | AI Content Creator, Prompts & AI Tools Tutorials`).
- **Meta Description**: High-converting, search-engine-friendly snippet targeting AI tutorials, free prompts, and tool stack.
- **Search Keywords**: Targeted keywords added (`DigiAi Dhairya`, `AI Content Creator`, `ChatGPT Prompts`, `Midjourney Prompts`, `AI Video Editing`, `Best AI Tools 2026`, `AI Tutorials in Gujarati / Hindi`).
- **Canonical Tag**: Defined canonical URL to prevent duplicate content issues.
- **Robots Directives**: Configured with `index, follow, max-image-preview:large`.
- **Open Graph (OG Tags)**: Full tags configured for WhatsApp, Facebook, and LinkedIn previews with high-quality preview image and descriptions.
- **Twitter / X Cards**: `summary_large_image` card configured for rich viral Twitter shares.
- **PWA Manifest (`manifest.json`)**: Configured for mobile app-like installation and branding.

### ✅ B. Structured Data Schema Markup (JSON-LD)
Implemented 4 Google-compliant schema graphs:
1. **`WebSite` Schema**: Gives Google full context on brand, url, and authority.
2. **`Person` Schema**: Connects Dhairya as the recognized AI Content Creator with verified social profiles (`sameAs` links to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook).
3. **`ItemList` Schema**: Outlines top AI tools (ChatGPT Plus, Midjourney, CapCut, ElevenLabs) for rich list indexing.
4. **`FAQPage` Schema**: Enables rich FAQ dropdown snippets directly in Google SERP results, dramatically boosting click-through rates (CTR).

### ✅ C. Search Engine Crawling & Indexing
- **`sitemap.xml`**: Lists all site anchors and pages with update frequency and priority.
- **`robots.txt`**: Grants search crawlers access and points directly to `sitemap.xml`.
